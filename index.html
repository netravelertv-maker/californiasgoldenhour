Mini-docs on the people and places that shaped the Golden State

California’s Golden Hour is a mini-documentary series about the landmarks, neighborhoods, beach towns, diners, rides and roadside icons that made California what it is.

Some stories celebrate places you can still visit today. Others remember the ones that are gone. Every episode is researched, narrated and built from archival photos, maps and footage.
